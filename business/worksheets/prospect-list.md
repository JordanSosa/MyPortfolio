## The outreach email

Short, specific, no attachment, no pitch. Send from a personal address, one at
a time, not a bulk tool.

> **Subject:** How you track `<the thing>`
>
> Hi `<name>` — I'm researching how `<type of business>` your size handle
> `<specific job>`. I'm not selling anything; I'm trying to work out whether a
> problem I keep hearing about is real.
>
> Would you have 15 minutes in the next fortnight? Happy to share what I find
> across all the firms I speak to.
>
> `<you>`

**Why it works:** it asks for a small, specific thing, it is honest about what
it is, and the offer to share findings is a genuine reason for them to say yes.

**One follow-up only**, 6 days later, two lines. Then stop — a third email
costs you the relationship for nothing.
