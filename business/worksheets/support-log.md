# Support Log — worksheet

One row per ticket. Two minutes a week to maintain. This is where your roadmap
comes from (`11`).

## The log

| Date | Customer | Category | Mins | Root cause | Fix layer | Fixed? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | | ☐ |

**Categories:** `onboarding` · `billing` · `bug` · `how-do-I` · `feature request` ·
`data/import` · `account` · `pre-sales` · `other`

**Fix layers** (`11`):
1. Product design — the question stops being asked
2. Docs / in-app help — they self-serve
3. Canned reply — you paste and personalise
4. Real thinking — genuinely novel

> **The rule:** every layer-4 ticket must produce an improvement at layer 1, 2
> or 3. A ticket you answer and forget is a ticket you will answer again.

**Target: under 5 minutes per customer per month.**

If it climbs for two months running, stop adding customers and fix the cause.
This is the metric that decides whether the business stays inside 10 hours a
week — everything else can look healthy while this one quietly turns it back
into a job.
