# Metrics — worksheet

One row per month, forever. Definitions and targets: [`../13-metrics-and-alerts.md`](../13-metrics-and-alerts.md).

Keep this in the repo. Over 24 months it becomes the history a buyer asks for
(`19`), and the commit dates are the proof it wasn't reconstructed the week
before the sale.

### Targets

| Metric | Target | Alarm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo churn | < 4%/mo | > 5% — this is the month's only priority |
| Trial → paid | 20–40% | < 15% — onboarding or positioning is broken |
| Support min/customer | < 5 | > 5 for two months — stop growing, start fixing |
| Traffic → signup | 2–5% | < 1% — wrong traffic or wrong offer |
| Gross margin | > 85% | < 80% — check per-unit COGS |
