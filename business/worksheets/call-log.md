# Call Log — worksheet

Ten conversations. Not surveys. Copy the block per call.

## Rules

- **Do not describe your idea until the last two minutes.** The moment you
  pitch, they start being polite, and politeness destroys the data.
- Ask about the *last time it happened*, not about what they generally do.
  Specific memories are true; generalisations are self-image.
- Silence is your best tool. Let them fill it.
- Write it up within an hour, or you'll keep the flattering half.

## The question script

1. Walk me through the last time you had to `<do the job>`. What actually happened?
2. What do you use for it today? How did you end up with that?
3. What's that costing you — licence, hours, whose hours?
4. When it goes wrong, what does wrong look like? When did it last go wrong?
5. What happens if nothing changes?
6. Who else touches this? Who'd decide on a new tool?
7. Have you looked for something better? What stopped you?
8. *(last two minutes)* I'm thinking about building `<outcome, not software>`. Does that land?
