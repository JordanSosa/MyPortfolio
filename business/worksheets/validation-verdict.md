## The gate

**Passed if:** 5+ deposits, **or** 3 signed paid pilots.

**Result:** ☐ Passed  ☐ Not passed

## Scoring honestly

| Result | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5+ deposits / 3 pilots | Real demand at a real price | Build → Phase 2 |
| 1–4 deposits | Something's there; offer or price is wrong | One more fortnight, changed offer, same market |
| Warm words, zero money | Pain real but not urgent or not budgeted | Re-scope to a sharper adjacent pain |
| No replies at all | Wrong list, wrong channel, or no pain | Back to `02` |

**Which row am I actually in?**

> The dangerous row is "warm words, zero money" — it feels like success and it
> is a no. If you are writing sentences here about how encouraging people were,
> you are in that row.
