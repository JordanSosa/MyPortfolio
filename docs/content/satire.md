# Satire bible

EXPANSION §12. The voice is an **over-excited anime narrator** plus **self-aware
gacha marketing copy**: short, punchy, quotable. We laugh at tropes (filler
episodes, transformation sequences, tsundere, chuunibyou, isekai trucks, gacha
fine print), never at people, never at real works, never sexualised. When in
doubt, the joke is on the format, the narrator, or the game itself.

Placeholders: `{hero}` hero name, `{stage}` stage name, `{boss}` boss name,
`{n}` episode number (lifetime run count + 1), `{time}` mm:ss, `{kills}`,
`{weapon}`. Pick lines with the run's seeded RNG (no repeats within 10 picks).

## 5. Pop rules (NANI?!, BAKA!, and friends)

Pops are pixel-lettered sprites (`fx_nani`, `fx_baka`, `fx_honk`) drawn in-world
on the text layer. Global rules for all pops: never drawn within 32 px of the
hero (shift away along the hit direction), never during the ult cut-in, scale
2.2 → 1.0 bounce + 8° wobble, 0.8 s life, max 3 pops on screen in total, all
disabled by the "Satire pops" setting (default on).

| Pop | Trigger | Chance | Cooldown | sfx |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NANI?!** (`fx_nani`, red-white, shaking) | a crit that deals ≥ 25% of the target's max HP, ≥ 500 damage, or kills an elite | 30% (Sparkle Screentone Sheet: 60%) | global 6 s; never 2 on screen | `nani` (vol 0.7) |
| **BAKA!** (`fx_baka` f0) | a Baka Harisen smack that hits ≥ 1 enemy; a Tsundere Slime's return; each Akane-ult smack | 35% per smack; ult always | global 1.2 s (the ult ignores it but caps at 1 / 0.25 s); max 2 on screen | `baka` |
| **...baka ♡** (`fx_baka` f1, small pink) | Dere Dere Harisen smacks | 35% | 1.2 s | `baka` (pitch −3, vol 0.5) |
| **HONK** (`fx_honk`) | Horn of Reincarnation honks, Mini Truck-kun lane starts | always | 1 s | `honk` |
| **Text pops** (font_main) | "Did I overdo it?", "PANIC HENSHIN!", "KIRA KIRA♡", "TAMAYA!", "HUG!", "CUT!", "PLOT HOLE FILLED!", "WISH GRANTED!" | per their weapon/boss | per their own rule | — |

Each pop shown counts into `RunStats.nanis` / `bakas` (achievements).

**Speech bubbles** (enemies): a white rounded bubble with a 1-px plum outline
and a tail, `font_main`, max 14 characters, 1.4 s. An enemy entering the view
has a 4% chance to speak (elites 25%; bosses only scripted lines); at most 3
bubbles on screen; a per-enemy-type cooldown of 8 s; no bubbles while more than
200 enemies are alive; never within 40 px of the hero.

## 10. Kira Kira Wiki blurbs (codex)

Wiki style: a one-line "trope" header, 2-3 sentences, a "Trivia" line. Shown
after discovery; kill counts are added by the UI ("Defeated: 1,204").

### 10.1 Dream Meadow
- **Puff** — *Trope: The Slime Is Always First.* A cheerful mint slime and the
  first enemy every hero meets. Hops toward you with tremendous enthusiasm and
  zero strategy. **Trivia:** the leaf on its head is decorative; it has asked us
  to stop asking.
- **Batbun** — *Trope: Fodder With Wings.* Half bunny, half bat, fully committed to
  flying straight into your weapons. Swarm events are their idea of a group
  project. **Trivia:** the ears are for steering. They do not steer.
- **Boo** — *Trope: The Shy Ghost.* Wobbles toward you on a sine wave, blushes, and
  says "boo" quietly. Flickers when nervous, which is always. **Trivia:** has
  never once scared anyone. Keeps trying.
- **Shroomie** — *Trope: Splitting Enemy.* A grumpy mushroom that splits into two
  Spores when defeated, out of spite. **Trivia:** the spots are not polka dots.
  Do not call them polka dots.
- **Spore** — *Trope: Mini Me.* Tiny, fast, furious, and very proud of being a
  Shroomie's whole legacy. **Trivia:** dreams of becoming a Shroomie. Never does.
- **Teddy Brute** — *Trope: The Tank.* A stitched-up teddy that walks slowly,
  hits hard and shrugs off knockback. Only wants a hug. Delivers it at
  speed. **Trivia:** the button eye was a gift from Nightmare Teddy.
- **Eyepuff** — *Trope: The Floating Eye.* A fluffball with one enormous anime eye
  that keeps its distance and fires glowing orbs. **Trivia:** blinks when it's
  about to shoot. Thinks you haven't noticed.
- **Pumpkin Dasher** — *Trope: The Charger.* Telegraphs its dash with a shake and a
  red flash, then commits to it completely. Skids. Every time. **Trivia:** has
  never once turned mid-dash. Considers it cheating.
- **Candle Wisp** — *Trope: Kamikaze Mook.* A tiny flame that rushes you and
  explodes. Its blast hurts other plushies too, which it considers a bonus.
  **Trivia:** the wax body is scented. The scent is "regret".

### 10.2 Candy Kingdom
- **Gummy Bear** — *Trope: Palette-Swap Squad.* Comes in four flavours and one
  personality: chewy. Boings back toward you after being knocked away.
  **Trivia:** the clear ones are "mystery flavour". The mystery is sadness.
- **Candy Corn Soldier** — *Trope: Faceless Army (With Faces).* Marches in
  formation and blocks shots from the front while its squad holds together.
  Break the formation, break the wall. **Trivia:** controversial among the
  kingdom's citizens. Loyal anyway.
- **Donut Roller** — *Trope: The Rolling Hazard.* Spins up, then rolls in a straight
  line, bouncing off the edge of the world once. **Trivia:** strawberry frosting.
  Always strawberry. Don't ask about the chocolate ones.
- **Marshmallow Brute** — *Trope: Big Soft Guy.* Heavy, slow and squishy. Fire
  toasts it: faster, angrier, and much easier to defeat. **Trivia:** the Toasty
  form is considered its "true form" by fans. It disagrees.
- **Lollipop Mage** — *Trope: The Healer Goes First.* Shoots curving candy and,
  every third cast, heals its sugary allies. Priority target. **Trivia:** the
  hat is a lollipop. The staff is a lollipop. The mage is not a lollipop. We think.

### 10.3 Shoujo Academy
- **Hall Monitor** — *Trope: Disciplinary Committee.* Ignores you until you dash
  nearby, then blows its whistle and charges. **Trivia:** has written 4,000
  tardy slips. 3,999 were for Batbuns.
- **Love Letter** — *Trope: Confession Scene.* Flaps toward you like a tiny paper
  bird. Sometimes bursts into persistent paper hearts when "rejected".
  **Trivia:** every letter is addressed to Senpai. None have arrived.
- **Tsundere Slime** — *Trope: Tsundere.* Gets hit, flees in embarrassment, then
  charges back faster ("B-baka!"). **Trivia:** the tiny drills are a tribute.
  Akane has not commented. Akane is furious.
- **Transfer Student** — *Trope: The Mysterious Transfer Student.* Never walks in;
  simply appears next to you with a sparkle and a dramatic scarf. Blinks around
  at regular intervals. **Trivia:** has transferred schools 47 times this season.
- **Exam Paper** — *Trope: Test Anxiety.* Flutters toward you and, on contact,
  lowers your grade (and your XP gain). **Trivia:** the F- is not a typo. The
  minus was earned.
- **Student Council Ninja** — *Trope: The Council's Shadow Squad.* Keeps its
  distance, throws shuriken, and smoke-bombs behind you if you get close.
  **Trivia:** the ninja club is officially a "stationery appreciation society".

### 10.4 Beach Episode
- **Crab-kun** — *Trope: The Beach Episode Mascot.* Only moves sideways, strafes
  around you, lunges when lined up and occasionally sidesteps a projectile.
  **Trivia:** the "-kun" was self-applied.
- **Seagull** — *Trope: Food Thief.* Circles, dives, and steals coins and snacks
  off the ground. Defeat it before it leaves to get them back. **Trivia:** has
  stolen 14,000 fries in its lifetime. There are no fries in this game.
- **Jellyfish** — *Trope: Environmental Hazard.* Drifts lazily and zaps the area
  around it every few seconds. When one goes down, nearby jellyfish feel it.
  **Trivia:** has no brain. Still has more strategy than the Batbuns.
- **Watermelon** — *Trope: Splits on Death.* Rolls at you, then splits into two
  angry halves that spit seeds. **Trivia:** wears a sun hat for sun safety.
  Respect that.
- **Watermelon Half** — *Trope: The Second Phase.* Half a melon, full of rage and
  seeds. **Trivia:** does not get along with the other half.
- **Sandcastle Golem** — *Trope: It Rebuilds Itself.* A tanky castle that rebuilds
  from its own sand pile unless you stomp the pile or blast it. **Trivia:** has
  a moat. The moat is decorative. The moat is also a puddle.
- **Tiny Shark** — *Trope: Fin in the Water (Sand).* Moves as just a fin, invisible
  to homing weapons, then surfaces with a "!" to chomp. **Trivia:** is a baby.
  Is very proud of its three teeth.

### 10.5 The Final Arc
- **Shadow Clone** — *Trope: Dark Mirror.* A dark copy of you with glowing red eyes
  that dashes whenever you do. **Trivia:** has your exact build, minus the
  personality.
- **Filler Episode Goon** — *Trope: Recycled Animation.* Interchangeable
  henchmen who all move on the same frame, and sometimes come back next episode.
  **Trivia:** has no lines in the script. Has asked for one. Was denied.
- **Plot Hole** — *Trope: Plot Hole.* A swirling tear in the storyboard that pulls
  you in and eats your gems and projectiles. Fill it to get the gems back.
  **Trivia:** nobody knows how it got there. That's the point.
- **Animation Error** — *Trope: Off-Model.* A badly drawn enemy that teleports in
  jerky frame-skips, with a hitbox smaller than its sprite. Leaves dropped
  frames behind. **Trivia:** fans have petitioned to make it canon. It is now
  canon.
- **Mini Truck-kun** — *Trope: Running Gag.* Telegraphs a lane, honks, and drives
  through it, flattening friend and foe alike. **Trivia:** Truck-kun's nephew.
  Learning the family business.
- **Rival (Recolour)** — *Trope: Evil Recolour.* Another hero with the colours
  swapped to red, sent by the Shounen Rival twist to "test your resolve".
  **Trivia:** the recolour was done in a hurry. You can tell.

### 10.6 Bosses
- **King Puff** — *Trope: The Tutorial Boss.* The ruler of the Squish Kingdom: hops,
  jumps, slams, summons. Wears a crown and a moustache. Both are real.
  **Trivia:** has been the first boss in every season. Considers it seniority.
- **Nightmare Teddy** — *Trope: Corrupted Cute Thing.* A huge ragged teddy with
  glowing stitches, charges, claw sweeps and star rings, angrier at half health.
  **Trivia:** has not slept since episode 1. It shows.
- **Lady Lunatic** — *Trope: The Elegant Final Boss.* Queen of the Endless Night:
  moon spirals, teleports, and a third phase that dims the whole world.
  **Trivia:** refers to the sunrise as "the incident".
- **Gingerbread Golem** — *Trope: Living Construct.* A gingerbread giant that stamps
  gingerbread-man-shaped attacks around you and loses a leg at half health.
  **Trivia:** was supposed to be a house. Has not processed this.
- **Sugar Plum Tyrant** — *Trope: The Sugar High Villain.* Pirouettes bullets,
  commands candy armies, and alternates sugar highs with sugar crashes.
  Strike during the crash. **Trivia:** has eaten nine desserts today. It's 10 a.m.
- **Student Council President** — *Trope: The Iron-Fisted President.* Stamps
  DENIED, glares lasers through her glasses and enforces School Regulations that
  change the rules of the fight. **Trivia:** Rule 21 is the only kind rule. She
  wrote it when she was tired.
- **The Bishounen Who Never Notices You** — *Trope: Unattainable Senpai.* Never aims
  at you — until his final phase, when he finally notices you and the whole
  world turns into a shoujo panel. **Trivia:** Fan club membership: 9,999. You
  applied. You were waitlisted.
- **Kraken-chan** — *Trope: The Misunderstood Monster.* A round, friendly octopus
  who slams tentacles, sprays ink and floods the arena — all because she wants a
  hug. **Trivia:** finally gets one in her defeat scene. From a crab.
- **Summer Festival Firework Dragon** — *Trope: The Festival Finale.* A paper-lantern
  dragon made of fireworks that turns the night sky into a bullet show.
  **Trivia:** has accidentally started the finale early every year.
- **Truck-kun** — *Trope: Truck-kun.* The legendary isekai truck. Changes lanes,
  backs up beeping, scatters traffic cones and brings friends at rush hour.
  **Trivia:** is also playable. Do not ask how. It finds a way.
- **Rival-chan** — *Trope: The Shounen Rival.* Has been your rival since episode 1;
  you do not remember her. Dashes, beams, a mid-fight training montage and a
  final beam clash. **Trivia:** she remembers every one of your 47 encounters.
  You remember none.
- **CANCEL-SAMA, the Network Executive** — *Trope: Executive Meddling.* The series
  finale boss: a suit with a TV for a head who drops the ratings, cuts the
  budget and rushes the ending — and the game degrades with every phase.
  **Trivia:** has never watched an episode. Has notes on all of them.
