# {business}: desk notes for drafting replies

## What the desk does
- ASIC corporate secretarial work for small accounting firms, done behind the firm: annual reviews (checking the annual statement, the solvency resolution, register updates), routine Form 484 changes (officeholder appointments and resignations, address changes), share issues and transfers, special resolutions and name changes, new companies and voluntary deregistrations.
- Acts as ASIC registered agent for the companies a firm moves to the desk (agent number {agent_number}). Transferring the agent to the desk is free.
- Doesn't do tax, BAS, bookkeeping or advice. The firm keeps the client relationship.
- Based in {location}. Serves firms Australia-wide.

## Service standard
- Standard ASIC changes are lodged within two business days of signed authority and any corporate key needed. The clock starts at signed authority, not at the first email.
- Documents usually go out for e-signing the same day the details are complete.
- Annual statements are retrieved within 14 days of each company's review date.

## Fees, all + GST, ASIC fees passed through at cost
- Desk fee: ${desk_fee} per company per year, billed monthly. Covers the annual review, solvency resolution, registers and routine 484 changes.
- Share issue or transfer: ${share_change_fee}.
- Special resolution or name change: ${special_resolution_fee}.
- New company: ${new_company_fee} plus the ASIC registration fee.
- Voluntary deregistration: ${deregistration_fee} plus the ASIC fee.
- Founding firms get {founding_discount} off the desk fee for the first 12 months on a 12-month term.

## How firms send work
- Changes come by email on the desk's one-page change template, one change per email. Anything missing is flagged in the first reply.

## Timing that matters to firms
- From 1 July 2027 companies start reporting director IDs, an electronic address and service addresses to ASIC, with full implementation by 1 August 2028. The desk can run that update across a firm's whole book.

## Data handling
- Australian-hosted storage, multi-factor authentication on every system, least-access handling.
